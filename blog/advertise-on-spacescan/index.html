Advertise on SpaceScan: reach the Chia community, paid in XCH

August 14, 2026 · 5 min read
Sabari
Founder

If you are building something on Chia, the hardest part is often not the building. It is getting the right people to see it.

SpaceScan now has self-serve banner advertising. Pick a size, upload a banner, pay in XCH or by card, and go live. No sales call, no contract, no minimum spend beyond a single month.

Who you are reaching​

People on SpaceScan are already looking at Chia on-chain data. They are checking a transaction, researching a token before buying, tracing an NFT's provenance, watching a wallet, or looking up which pool won a block.

That is a narrow audience, and that is the point. It is Chia holders, farmers, traders, developers and project teams, at the moment they are paying attention to the chain.

What you can buy​

Two banner sizes, one shared slot in each, rotating evenly between advertisers.

SizeWhere it appears
320 x 100Beside the search on the homepage, and at the top of pages like blocks, rich list, pre-farm, DataLayer, pools and farmers
728 x 90Full-width at the bottom of every page on the explorer, the widest reach we sell

Introductory price: 1 XCH per day, so 30 XCH for 30 days. Same flat rate for both sizes, and it holds until September 30, 2026, the same date as our Pro launch offer. After that, banner slots move to our standard rate of $99 a month.

Most Chia projects hold coins rather than dollars, so XCH is the native rail, and the introductory rate lives there: buy before September 30 and you pay 30 XCH for that full month. Prefer a card? Pay the standard $99 by card today, through Stripe.

How to buy it, step by step​

The whole thing lives at spacescan.io/advertise.

  1. Sign in. You need an account so you can manage the campaign later and see how it is doing.
  2. Choose your size. 320 x 100 or 728 x 90.
  3. Upload your banner. PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 4 MB. You will see a preview at the real size before you commit.
  4. Set your link. Where the banner should send people.
  5. Pay. In XCH, we generate a one-time payment address for your order, and the payment is picked up as soon as it is seen on-chain. By card, Stripe handles checkout.
  6. We review it. Usually within a day.
  7. It goes live. Your 30 days start when the banner is approved, not when you paid, so time spent in review does not come out of your slot.

What the review actually checks​

Every creative is reviewed by a person before it serves. We are checking that the banner is honest about what it is advertising, that it is not adult content, and that it is not impersonating another project or pretending to be part of SpaceScan.

If something needs changing, the campaign shows Changes needed with a note explaining what to fix. Upload a new banner and it goes back into the queue. Nothing is lost.

You can watch the whole thing from the same page. A campaign moves through:

Awaiting payment → In review → Live

Once it is live, you see your own view and click counts on that page.

Changing your banner later​

Upload a new creative any time. It goes back through review, and here is the part worth knowing: your current banner keeps running until the new one is approved. There is no dark period where your slot sits empty while someone looks at your new artwork.

Being straight with you​

We sell by the month, not by impressions. Our traffic measurement is still being corrected after the rebuild, and we will not quote an audience number we cannot stand behind.

The slot is shared. Banners rotate evenly between advertisers, and our own house banners take one share of that rotation.

We count campaigns, not people. No profiles, no targeting to buy.

Refunds are manual. Email us and we will sort it out properly.

We will not run anything misleading, adult, or impersonating another project.

A cheaper option, if you just want to be seen​

If what you actually want is your token or NFT collection in front of people, a banner may be the wrong purchase. A featured listing pins your token or collection to the top of the relevant list with a Promoted label. Nothing to design, and it costs less than a banner.

Get started​

Everything is at spacescan.io/advertise.

Questions, or something bigger than a banner in mind? Email hello@spacescan.io or find us on Discord.

Advertising is one of the ways we keep the core explorer free for everyone. Blocks, transactions, tokens, NFTs, offers and farming data stay open, with no account and no paywall. Thank you to everyone who supports SpaceScan, whether by advertising, subscribing to Pro, or simply using it every day.
