If you are building something on Chia, the hardest part is often not the building. It is getting the right people to see it.

SpaceScan now has self-serve banner advertising. Pick a size, upload a banner, pay in XCH, and go live. No sales call, no contract, no minimum spend beyond a single month.

<!-- truncate -->

## Who you are reaching

People on SpaceScan are already looking at Chia on-chain data. They are checking a transaction, researching a token before buying, tracing an NFT's provenance, watching a wallet, or looking up which pool won a block.

That is a narrow audience, and that is the point. It is Chia holders, farmers, traders, developers and project teams, at the moment they are paying attention to the chain.

## What you can buy

Two banner sizes, one shared slot in each, rotating evenly between advertisers.

| Size | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **320 x 100** | Beside the search on the homepage, and at the top of pages like blocks, rich list, pre-farm, DataLayer, pools and farmers |
| **728 x 90** | Full-width at the bottom of every page on the explorer, the widest reach we sell |

**Price: 1 XCH per day, so 30 XCH for 30 days.** Same flat price for both sizes. Payment is in XCH, because Chia projects hold coins rather than dollars.

## How to buy it, step by step

The whole thing lives at [spacescan.io/advertise](https://spacescan.io/advertise).

1. **Sign in.** You need an account so you can manage the campaign later and see how it is doing.
2. **Choose your size.** 320 x 100 or 728 x 90.
3. **Upload your banner.** PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 4 MB. You will see a preview at the real size before you commit.
4. **Set your link.** Where the banner should send people.
5. **Pay in XCH.** We generate a one-time payment address for your order. Send the coins and the payment is picked up as soon as it is seen on-chain.
6. **We review it.** Usually within a day.
7. **It goes live.** Your 30 days start when the banner is approved, not when you paid, so time spent in review does not come out of your slot.

## What the review actually checks

Every creative is reviewed by a person before it serves. We are checking that the banner is honest about what it is advertising, that it is not adult content, and that it is not impersonating another project or pretending to be part of SpaceScan.

If something needs changing, the campaign shows **Changes needed** with a note explaining what to fix. Upload a new banner and it goes back into the queue. Nothing is lost.

You can watch the whole thing from the same page. A campaign moves through:

**Awaiting payment** → **In review** → **Live**

Once it is live, you see your own view and click counts on that page.

## Changing your banner later

Upload a new creative any time. It goes back through review, and here is the part worth knowing: **your current banner keeps running until the new one is approved.** There is no dark period where your slot sits empty while someone looks at your new artwork.

## Being straight with you

A few things we would rather say up front than have you discover later.

**We sell by the month, not by impressions.** We are not going to quote you an audience number we cannot stand behind. Our traffic measurement is still being corrected after the rebuild, and selling views we cannot honestly count is worse than selling nothing. You are buying a slot for a month, on a site whose audience you can judge for yourself.

**The slot is shared, not exclusive.** Banners rotate evenly between advertisers, so with two advertisers you each get half the views. Our own house banners also take one share of the rotation, which shrinks as more slots sell.

**We count campaigns, not people.** Views and clicks are counted per campaign. We do not build profiles of readers, and there is no targeting to buy, because we are not collecting the data that would make targeting possible.

**Refunds are manual and case by case.** We are a small team. If something goes wrong, email us and we will sort it out properly, but there is no automated refund button.

**What we will not run:** anything misleading, adult content, or creatives impersonating another project.
